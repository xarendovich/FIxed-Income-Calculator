# Adjudication: the alignment review of 2026-10-08

- **Asked:** a reviewer proposed six modifications "to keep the standard pattern strictly aligned with Spark-core functions and the zero-action restraints of the `ddec911` Kernel-Update." The owner asked Claude to deliberate them.
- **Standing:** Claude's verdicts, subject to the owner. Where a verdict modifies a recommendation, the evidence is stated. Accepted items are applied in this commit; items that change the contract or need the owner's word are recorded, not done.
- **One framing correction first.** "Zero-action restraint" is this pattern's invariant I-1, not a rule of the kernel register; the kernel at `ddec911` moves daemon concerns *out* (KU-07, KU-20) and takes no dependency on any daemon. Aligning with it means staying below it, which the pattern does. The review's target is right; its attribution is reversed.
